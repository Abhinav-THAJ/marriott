Clear Answers

Frequently Addressed International Inquiries

How fast can an emergency air ambulance be dispatched? expand_more

Our critical care flight team operates 24/7 in partnership with certified global aeromedical providers. For regional locations (GCC, South Asia, East Africa), pressurized fixed-wing ICU aircraft can be activated within 4 to 8 hours following fit-to-fly clearance.

What documents are needed for an urgent Indian Medical Visa (e-Med)? expand_more

You only need to submit the patient and attendant passport copies alongside recent medical summaries. Our International Patient Bureau issues the formal Ministry-compliant Medical Visa Invitation Letter on hospital letterhead within 4 hours.

How does Sanctuary Health manage foreign health insurance & embassies? expand_more

We maintain direct billing relationships with major international insurers including Bupa Global, Cigna Global, Allianz Care, Aetna International, as well as health ministries and military attachés across Oman, UAE, Bahrain, Kuwait, and African national insurance boards.

Can my local home doctor participate in the collegiate tumor board? expand_more

Yes. We encourage referring physicians to attend the live video discussion. A secure encrypted tele-conferencing link is provided along with real-time screen sharing of high-resolution radiology and digital pathology slides.
